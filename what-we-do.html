Our process

One five-stage executive search process, every mandate.

Brief

Founder-led briefing with the hiring leadership. We pressure-test the role definition and align on profile, geographies and total comp envelope.

Research

Market map of qualified senior candidates across the relevant geographies. Off-limits validated. Outreach sequencing agreed.

Longlist

A longlist presentation with written rationale per candidate, reviewed with you before any candidate moves into formal interview.

Shortlist

Structured competency-led interviews completed. Reference triangulation underway. A 3–5 person shortlist with a clear recommendation.

Placement

Guided offer process, references closed, counter-offer management and a 12-week onboarding check-in.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions about our search work.

A retained executive search is an exclusive engagement in which the search firm is paid in stages — typically a third on engagement, a third on shortlist and a third on placement — to run a thorough, market-mapped process. Retained search is the standard for genuine executive-level mandates because it allows a headhunter to invest the research, qualification and relationship-building time that contingent recruitment cannot.
Most senior AdTech executive search firms charge a retained fee of 25–33% of the placed candidate's first-year cash compensation. Progressive People works on a retained, exclusive basis with a transparent staged fee structure agreed up-front in the engagement letter.
A typical senior commercial search — for example a CRO or Chief Commercial Officer — completes shortlist in 6–8 weeks and reaches offer within 10–14 weeks. Highly specialist or international searches can extend to 16–20 weeks depending on candidate availability and the level of confidentiality required.
