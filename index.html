Who we are

A boutique headhunting partner for the people who build advanced media businesses.

Founded by Scott Deutrom and Keith Rattray — two senior commercial leaders with over 40 years of combined digital media leadership experience — Progressive People exists because the AdTech, advanced TV and digital media industries need a search partner who genuinely understands them.

We have a deep understanding of the digital media landscape, from traditional media brands to the large AdTech platforms, through to the emerging technologies and disruptors. Despite the proliferation of automated optimisation technologies, self-serve platforms and the emergence of AI/ML, brilliant talent and leadership will always be required to fully exploit the potential of businesses serving the sector.

As one of the UK's leading AdTech executive search firms, we work on a retained, exclusive basis with founders, boards and global leadership teams — identifying, qualifying and securing the senior commercial and technical talent that defines a company's next chapter.

Sectors we serve

Six sectors. One specialism: senior advanced-media talent.

AdTech

SSPs, DSPs, ad servers, identity & consent, attribution, verification, curation and retail media platforms. We place commercial, product and engineering leadership for the businesses defining the AdTech stack.

Programmatic

Programmatic trading, yield, AdOps, biddable media and demand leadership across agencies, publishers and platforms. Senior programmatic recruitment built around real domain fluency.

Advanced TV & CTV

Addressable, CTV, BVOD, FAST channels and the convergence of linear and digital. Advanced TV talent acquisition for broadcasters, streamers and the platforms enabling them.

Publishers & Media Owners

Commercial, supply-side and audience leadership for publishers, broadcasters, newsbrands and pure-play digital media owners navigating identity and signal loss.

Media Sales

Sales Directors, Country Managers, Agency Heads and senior individual contributors building revenue lines for media businesses across the UK, EU and US markets.

MarTech & Data

CDPs, clean rooms, identity graphs, measurement and analytics. We headhunt the leaders bridging the AdTech and MarTech worlds for enterprise and growth-stage businesses.
