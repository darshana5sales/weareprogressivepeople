Who we are

A boutique headhunting partner for the people who scale advanced media businesses.

Founded by Scott Deutrom and Keith Rattray — two senior commercial leaders with over 40 years of combined digital media leadership experience — Progressive People exists because the AdTech, advanced TV and digital media industries need a search partner who genuinely understands them.

We have a deep understanding of the advanced media landscape, from traditional media brands to the large AdTech platforms, through to the emerging technologies and disruptors. Despite the proliferation of automated optimisation technologies, self-serve platforms and the emergence of AI/ML, brilliant talent and leadership will always be required to fully exploit the potential of businesses serving the sector.

As one of the UK's leading AdTech executive search firms, we partner with founders, boards and leadership teams on the appointments that matter most — from the senior commercial and technical hires that set a company's direction, to the operational and management roles that turn ambition into scale. We work on both a retained and contingent basis, matching the engagement to the brief rather than prescribing every client to the same model.
