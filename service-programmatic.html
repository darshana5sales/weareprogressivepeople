Senior programmatic recruitment

Programmatic executive recruiters with real domain fluency.

The programmatic world has changed enormously since auction dynamics first replaced direct insertion orders. Bidstream optimisation, supply-path management, identity solutions, curated marketplaces, retail media and CTV programmatic have each created new specialisms — and new leadership profiles to qualify against.

A senior programmatic recruitment search demands a partner who can tell the difference between a Head of Programmatic who genuinely built a trading function from scratch and one who simply inherited one. We do that work for our clients — the qualification, the reference triangulation, the structured competency interview — before any candidate enters a formal shortlist.

For candidates, that same depth means we can talk credibly about the programmatic advertising jobs worth moving for, across London, the wider UK and the EU.

Programmatic disciplines we cover

From buy-side trading to supply-side yield, we headhunt across the full programmatic spectrum.

  • Buy-side programmatic trading and biddable media leadership
  • Supply-side yield, AdOps and demand operations
  • Curation, deal-ID strategy and supply-path optimisation
  • CTV / addressable programmatic and OTT supply
  • Retail media programmatic and commerce media
  • Programmatic strategy, consulting and transformation

For candidates: programmatic advertising jobs in London & beyond

As a retained executive search firm, we headhunt senior programmatic roles rather than running an open job board. If you are a Head of Programmatic, Trading Director, Yield lead or AdOps leader exploring your next move, sharing your profile confidentially means we can match you to the right mandate when it lands — in London, across the UK or into EU territories.

Everything you share stays with the founders, and we never approach the market or share your CV without your explicit permission.
