Cookie Policy.

Last updated 2 November 2026 Version 1.1

This policy describes the site as currently built. At the time of writing, weareprogressivepeople.com sets no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies of its own. If analytics, a chat widget, an embedded video or a marketing pixel is added later, this page must be updated and a consent banner added — under PECR those cookies may only be set after the visitor has actively consented.

This policy explains how this website uses cookies and similar technologies, what they do, and how you can control them. It sits alongside our Privacy Policy, which explains how we handle personal data more generally.

1. What cookies are

A cookie is a small text file that a website asks your browser to store on your device. Cookies are used to make a site work, to remember a preference between visits, or to measure how a site is used. Similar technologies — local storage, session storage and tracking pixels — do comparable jobs and are treated the same way in this policy.

Cookies set by the site you are visiting are called first-party cookies. Cookies set by another domain whose content is embedded in the page are third-party cookies.

2. The rules that apply

In the UK, cookies are governed by the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR), read alongside the UK GDPR. In short: cookies that are strictly necessary for a service you have asked for can be set without consent, and everything else — analytics, advertising, personalisation, social embeds — requires your prior, informed, opt-in consent. Continuing to browse is not consent, and pre-ticked boxes are not consent.

3. What this website currently uses

This is a deliberately lightweight site. It does not run an analytics package, an advertising pixel, a chat widget or a social media tracker, and it does not set any cookie for marketing or measurement purposes.

Strictly necessary Minimal No first-party cookies are set to browse this site. If a cookie is later required to secure a form submission against abuse, it will be listed here.

Analytics / performance No No analytics cookies are set. If analytics is introduced, it will be listed here and will only run after consent.

4. Third-party content and requests

Three things on this site involve a request to another organisation. The first two set no cookies. The third, our booking calendar, only loads if you ask for it.

  • Google Fonts. Our typefaces are served from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com. Loading a font does not set a cookie, but it does mean your IP address and browser details are visible to Google in order to deliver the file. Google’s handling of that data is covered by its own privacy notice.
  • LinkedIn. Our footer and contact page link to our LinkedIn company page. These are ordinary links, not embedded widgets, so nothing is set unless you choose to follow the link — at which point LinkedIn’s own cookie policy applies.
  • Calendly (booking calendar). Our contact page offers an intro-call booking calendar provided by Calendly. Nothing is loaded from Calendly until you click Show available times. Once it loads, Calendly receives your IP address and browser details and may set its own cookies, including those needed to run the booking. Any details you enter to book a call are collected by Calendly on our behalf. Calendly’s handling of that data is covered by its own privacy notice. If you would rather not use it, email us and we will arrange a time directly.

If you would prefer the site not to contact Google at all, self-hosting the font files removes that request entirely. [Option for the client to consider.]

5. Server logs

Like almost every website, our host records standard technical information when a page is requested — IP address, the page requested, timestamp, browser type and referring page. This is not done with cookies. It is used to keep the site available and secure and to diagnose faults, and is retained for a short period only.

6. Forms and newsletter sign-up

Our contact form, CV submission form and newsletter sign-up collect the information you type into them. That is personal data rather than a cookie, and how we use it is set out in our Privacy Policy. Subscribing to the newsletter is opt-in, and every email carries a one-click unsubscribe link.

7. How to control cookies

You can block or delete cookies through your browser at any time. Because we set none for tracking, doing so will not affect how this site works for you — though blocking third-party cookies may stop the Calendly booking calendar from loading, in which case you can email us to arrange a call. The relevant settings are here:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

Most browsers also offer a private or incognito mode, which discards cookies and site data when the window is closed.

8. Changes to this policy

If we add analytics, embedded media or any other technology that sets a non-essential cookie, we will update this page and introduce a consent mechanism before it runs. The version number and date at the top of this page show when it was last revised.

9. Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email scott@weareprogressivepeople.com, or use our contact form. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.
