Media tech headhunters London

Where media meets technology — and where the senior commercial talent sits.

Media tech is a highly adjacent, high-volume world to AdTech — and the leaders who succeed in it understand both the editorial and the engineering side. As media tech headhunters in London, we sit inside that network, with founder-level experience spanning broadcast, publishing and AdTech.

Media sales director recruitment sits at the heart of this. Whether the mandate is a Sales Director leading a national publisher team, a Country Manager opening a new EMEA territory for a US platform, or a Head of Agency at a broadcaster, the brief is the same: a commercial leader with the track record, network and judgement to build revenue in a market that is structurally changing year on year.

We do not work from a database. We work from market mapping, peer-validated reputation and structured competency interviews refined over more than two decades of placing commercial leaders in advanced media.

Advanced TV & CTV talent acquisition

Advanced television — addressable TV, connected TV (CTV), BVOD, FAST channels and OTT — is the part of the media industry undergoing the most rapid leadership change. With deep founder-level experience across broadcast and AdTech, we are regularly retained by broadcasters, streamers, FAST operators and the platforms enabling them to find leaders who can navigate the convergence of linear and digital.

  • Broadcaster commercial, digital and addressable leadership
  • FAST channel and AVOD platform leadership
  • CTV / OTT advertising platform sales and product leadership
  • Streaming, SVOD and hybrid-monetisation commercial teams
  • Set-top-box, smart-TV and device-level advertising businesses

Media sales director recruitment

Our advanced TV and media sales leadership work in London — on remits that increasingly reach across EMEA and beyond — is built on a deep, current network of senior commercial talent.
