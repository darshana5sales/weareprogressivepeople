CRO & commercial leadership recruitment

Revenue leadership is the hire a scaling media business gets wrong most expensively.

Commercial leadership recruitment covers the roles that carry a number: the Chief Revenue Officer who owns the plan, the Sales Director who builds the team that delivers it, the Country Manager who opens a market, and the Agency Head who owns the relationships the plan depends on.

These are the hardest senior hires to assess from a CV. Two candidates can both have carried a £20m number and be entirely different propositions — one inherited a mature book in a category with tailwinds, the other built a new revenue line from a standing start against incumbents. Our founders have run commercial teams inside media and AdTech businesses, so we qualify against the actual job rather than the job description.

We work on both a retained and contingent basis, matching the engagement to the brief. A confidential incumbent replacement or a first CRO appointment is retained work. A well-defined sales leadership hire in a liquid part of the market often is not, and we will say so.

Chief Revenue Officer and commercial leadership search

A Chief Revenue Officer search is rarely a search for a better salesperson. It is a search for the operating model the business needs next — founder-led selling converted into a repeatable go-to-market engine, a single-product revenue line broadened into a platform proposition, or a domestic business turned into a regional one.

That is why our CRO recruitment starts with a briefing that usually reframes the brief. Before we approach anyone, we agree what the first four quarters actually look like, which parts of the number are new business versus retention, what the leader inherits in terms of team and tooling, and where the realistic total compensation envelope sits against the market.

From there the search is a structured process: a market map of qualified commercial leaders, direct approaches to passive candidates, competency-led interviews against the agreed operating model, reference triangulation beyond the names the candidate offers, and a guided offer process.

Media sales leadership for broadcasters, publishers and platforms

Media sales leadership sits inside this discipline. Sales Directors, Heads of Sales, Agency Heads and senior individual contributors build the revenue lines for media businesses navigating identity loss, audience fragmentation and the shift to addressable and programmatic monetisation.

The commercial leader who thrives in that environment is fluent in more than the sell. They understand how the inventory is packaged, how the trading desks and agency groups actually buy, where the yield sits, and how a direct relationship and a programmatic deal ID relate to each other on the same account. We qualify for that fluency explicitly.

UK-based leaders, single-market to global remits

Progressive People is a UK-based practice. The commercial leaders we place are typically UK-based, sometimes EMEA-based — and their remits are what reach further. A Sales Director may own a single market. A Country Manager may own a territory. A CRO or SVP may carry a regional EMEA number, or a global one.

We are explicit about this distinction because it matters to both sides. A candidate weighing a move needs to know whether the role is a London number or an EMEA one, and a client needs a partner who is honest about where their network genuinely runs deep rather than claiming presence everywhere.
