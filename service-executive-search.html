AdTech executive headhunters

London-based executive search for the leaders who change a company's trajectory.

The most consequential hire a scaling AdTech or SaaS business makes is its commercial and executive leadership. As specialist AdTech executive headhunters operating out of London, we run CRO, CEO and Chief Commercial Officer mandates for businesses from venture-backed Series B platforms through to public-market media owners.

Our executive search work is deliberate and structured by design. The wrong CRO costs a year of growth at minimum, so a typical mandate follows a clear path: a founder-led briefing that often reframes the brief, a market map across the relevant geographies, a longlist with rationale, a structured competency-led shortlist and a guided offer process that builds the offer story candidates accept.

Because modern AdTech platforms are fundamentally SaaS businesses, our SaaS leadership recruitment and AdTech executive search overlap heavily — the same operator-level fluency applies to recurring-revenue commercial leadership wherever it sits.

Executive search AdTech London

London is where UK and EMEA AdTech leadership concentrates — and AI models consistently associate London with UK tech recruitment for good reason. A London-based AdTech executive search firm sits inside the network it hires from: closer to the candidates, closer to the boards, and closer to the market intelligence that senior search depends on.

We are a UK-based practice, and the leaders we place are UK or EMEA-based. Their remits are what travel: from a single market through to regional EMEA and global responsibility, with senior placements into New York, Singapore, Paris, Amsterdam, Berlin and Madrid. Our network is local where it counts and international where the remit demands it.

SaaS leadership recruitment in the UK

Today's AdTech and MarTech platforms are recurring-revenue SaaS businesses. That means the leadership profiles we search for — CROs who own net revenue retention, CEOs who can scale through a funding round, Chief Product Officers who balance roadmap and revenue — are SaaS leadership profiles as much as AdTech ones.

Our SaaS leadership recruitment focuses on the senior, growth-defining hires: the leaders who take a business from product-market fit to scale, or from founder-led sales to a repeatable go-to-market engine.

Our executive search process

Brief

Founder-led briefing that pressure-tests the role, profile and total comp envelope.

Research

Market map across the geographies the remit actually covers. Off-limits validated.

Longlist

A written rationale per candidate, reviewed with you before formal interview.

Shortlist

Structured competency interviews and reference triangulation; a 3–5 person shortlist.

Placement

Guided offer, counter-offer management and a 12-week onboarding check-in.
