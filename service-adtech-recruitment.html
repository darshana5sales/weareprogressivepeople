AdTech recruitment agency UK

An AdTech recruitment agency built by AdTech operators.

Most firms that offer AdTech recruitment in the UK are generalists with an AdTech vertical. Progressive People is the opposite — a boutique AdTech recruitment agency whose founders built their careers inside the AdTech and digital media industries before moving into executive search.

That distinction matters most at the senior end. A Chief Revenue Officer who has scaled a programmatic SSP is solving a fundamentally different problem from a CRO at a traditional media owner, and only AdTech executive headhunters who understand that difference will run a search that ends in a placement that lasts. We qualify senior candidates the way a CRO would, not the way a generalist would.

We work on both a retained and contingent basis with founders, boards and leadership teams — identifying, qualifying and securing the senior AdTech talent that defines a company's next chapter, whether that is a single C-suite hire or a leadership team build-out ahead of funding or expansion.

Senior AdTech recruitment across the full stack

We headhunt across the entire AdTech ecosystem. Whether you are a supply-side platform scaling a commercial team, a DSP hiring its first Chief Product Officer, or a retail media network building leadership from scratch, our AdTech recruitment work covers the businesses defining the category.

  • Supply-side platforms (SSPs), ad exchanges and curation
  • Demand-side platforms (DSPs), trading desks and biddable media
  • Ad serving, identity, consent management and privacy infrastructure
  • Attribution, measurement, MMM and brand-safety / verification
  • Connected TV, addressable, OTT and BVOD platforms
  • Retail media, commerce media and on-site monetisation
  • MarTech and CDPs at the intersection with AdTech

Why our AdTech executive headhunters outperform generalists

Senior AdTech recruitment lives or dies on the quality of qualification. The best candidate for your role is almost never looking, and will not respond to a generic outreach. Our AdTech executive headhunters reach them through market mapping, peer-validated reputation and a credible, operator-level conversation — not a database keyword search.

Because we are a boutique AdTech recruitment agency with a small client list, our off-limits list is short. Most of the senior AdTech leaders a global firm cannot approach for your role, we can. And because every search is run personally by a founder, the person you brief is the person who closes the candidate.

How an AdTech search runs

Every AdTech recruitment mandate follows the same five-stage, retained process — refined across two decades of senior placement work in advanced media.

Brief

Founder-led briefing that pressure-tests the role, profile, geography and total comp envelope.

Research

Market map of qualified senior AdTech candidates. Off-limits validated, outreach agreed.

Longlist

A written rationale per candidate, reviewed with you before any formal interview.

Shortlist

Structured competency interviews and reference triangulation; a 3–5 person shortlist.

Placement

Guided offer, counter-offer management and a 12-week onboarding check-in.
