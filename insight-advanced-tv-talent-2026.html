Advanced TV talent in 2026: where the senior CTV commercial leaders actually sit.

By Scott Deutrom 12 February 2026 9 min read
On this page
  • Four pools
  • What convergence has done to the job
  • What this means for hiring
  • Where these people are based

Advanced TV is the part of the advanced media market where demand for senior commercial leadership most obviously exceeds supply. It is also the part where clients most consistently look in the wrong places.

The instinct is to search the streamers. It is understandable and it is usually unproductive: the population is small, well-paid, recently hired and heavily off-limits to the larger search firms. The more useful question is where the experience was actually built.

Four pools, four different profiles

1. The broadcasters. UK broadcast sales houses remain the deepest training ground for advanced TV commercial leadership in this market. People who ran addressable propositions inside a broadcaster understand the thing almost nobody else does: how to grow digital revenue without detonating the linear business that still funds the content. That is a political and commercial skill as much as a technical one, and it is the single most transferable capability in the sector.

2. The platform and adtech side. Leaders from CTV ad servers, SSPs with strong video inventory, and the measurement and verification businesses serving them. This pool is technically strong and commercially credible with buyers, and it is where you find people who can talk to a broadcaster's ad ops team without an interpreter. What it sometimes lacks is experience carrying a large direct revenue number.

3. The FAST and AVOD operators. A younger pool, but the operators who launched and scaled FAST channels have done something genuinely relevant: built a monetisation model from nothing, under scrutiny, with limited inventory and no brand inheritance. For a growth-stage business this profile is frequently a better fit than a broadcaster veteran.

4. The agency investment and video specialists. Senior agency-side video and investment leaders understand exactly how advanced TV is bought, which is what a sell-side business is ultimately hiring for. The transition from buy side to sell side is real but well-trodden, and these candidates are often overlooked because their titles do not contain the letters CTV.

The most transferable capability in advanced TV is knowing how to grow digital revenue without detonating the linear business that funds the content.

What convergence has done to the job

The reason these pools are now interchangeable in a way they were not five years ago is that the underlying job has converged. A senior advanced TV commercial leader in 2026 is expected to hold linear, BVOD, CTV and FAST in one commercial narrative; to sell audiences rather than spots; to have a defensible position on cross-platform measurement; and to run a proposition that is bought both directly and programmatically, often by the same client in the same quarter.

Very few people have done all of that. Almost everyone credible has done three of the four, which is why the profile decision at brief stage matters so much.

What this means for hiring

  • Specify the gap, not the title. “Director of Advanced TV” describes a title held by people with entirely different skills. Specify whether the hire is there to build a proposition, sell one, or fix the measurement story.
  • Treat CTV-badged experience with some scepticism. Three years at a streamer during a growth phase is not the same as having built something. Ask what existed before they arrived.
  • Expect to pay a premium, and know why. This population is small and being approached constantly. The compensation conversation should be had at brief stage.
  • Consider the adjacent pool seriously. The strongest advanced TV shortlists we build usually contain at least one candidate whose current title does not mention television.

Where these people are based

The UK market is unusually well served here. London concentrates broadcaster, platform, agency and FAST experience in a way few markets do, and the leaders we place are overwhelmingly UK-based. What has changed is the scope of the roles they take: remits that were once domestic now routinely carry EMEA responsibility, and occasionally global. That is a shift in the job, not in where the talent lives.

We run advanced TV and CTV search for broadcasters, streamers and the platforms enabling convergence. If you are building a team in this space, brief our team.
