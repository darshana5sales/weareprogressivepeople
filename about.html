About us

Progressive People is a boutique, retained AdTech recruitment and executive search firm. We were founded in 2021 by two senior commercial leaders — Scott Deutrom and Keith Rattray — who between them have over 40 years of combined digital media leadership experience inside the businesses we now headhunt for.

Our story

Built by operators. Run as a boutique. Trusted as a partner.

Progressive People exists because the AdTech, advanced TV and digital media industries deserved a search partner with genuine, founder-level depth in the markets they hire from.

Scott Deutrom spent more than twenty years leading domestic and multi-territory digital teams across some of the UK and EU's most successful commercial properties — spanning TV, VoD, national press, magazines and AdTech brands — through a period of unprecedented growth, transformation and consolidation. Keith Rattray began his career as deputy Head of Broadcast at MG OMD and Head of Digital at Carat UK, then founded dot-gap in 2006 — one of the UK's leading buy-side recruitment practices — which he ran for fifteen years before co-founding Progressive People.

The thesis was straightforward. The biggest headhunter firms had AdTech verticals; very few had AdTech operators. The largest firms could mobilise globally; very few could speak to a CRO about supply-path optimisation with credibility. We built Progressive People to fill that gap — AdTech executive headhunters for the advanced media industry, run by the people who built careers inside it.

Why a boutique partner

A different model for senior executive search.

Founder-led, every mandate

Every search is run personally by Scott or Keith. There is no junior researcher relay, no offshored qualification call, no associate handover. The person you brief is the person who closes the candidate.

Retained & exclusive only

We do not run contingent mandates. As a retained headhunter, our economic model is built around investing the time genuine executive search demands — market mapping, qualification, reference triangulation.

Short off-limits list

A small client list means a short off-limits list. Most of the candidates a global firm cannot approach for your role, a private headhunter like Progressive People can.

Operator-level fluency

Our founders have actually run commercial teams inside AdTech and digital media businesses. We qualify candidates the way a CRO would, not the way a generalist would.

International by default

As one of the more internationally networked boutique headhunters in the UK market, we routinely run searches across the UK, EU and US — with senior placements in New York, Singapore, Paris, Amsterdam, Berlin and Madrid.

Confidential by design

A material proportion of our work is confidential — incumbent replacements, pre-funding hires and competitive replacements. We never speak to the market without explicit, scoped authority from our client.

What we believe

Four principles that shape every search.

01

Judgement over volume

Our value is in saying no — to roles we should not take, to candidates who are not right, to processes that will not finish well.

02

Operator-level qualification

We qualify senior candidates against the actual job, not against the JD. That difference is why placements stick.

03

Respect for both sides

Candidates are not commodities. Clients are not transactions. The senior advanced-media industry is small enough that this is also commercially obvious.

04

Long-term > short-term

We would rather lose a mandate to a competitor than place someone we know will not last twelve months. Reputation compounds; volume does not.

Why our approach is different

A small team is the point, not a constraint.

Most of the executive search firms founders consider when hiring senior advanced-media talent are global generalists. They have AdTech verticals, programmatic verticals, TV verticals — but the work is run by people who learned the market from the outside.

Progressive People is the opposite. Two senior AdTech executive headhunters, both of whom built careers inside the businesses we now search for, running every mandate personally. We are deliberately small. We do not have a junior researcher tier. We do not split a mandate across three offices. We do not pretend to have local depth in markets we have not personally worked in.

For the right client, that is the entire value proposition: when you brief Scott or Keith, you are briefing the person who will personally market-map your role, personally approach the top thirty candidates, personally qualify the shortlist and personally close the offer. There is no relay.

It is also why we limit the number of active mandates we take on. A boutique headhunter that runs sixty live searches a year is not really a boutique. Our cap is materially lower than that, and it shows up in candidate quality. Beyond the founders, we work with a small group of senior advisors and specialist associates — market researchers, compensation specialists and former operators — on a per-mandate basis, introduced at the briefing stage where their expertise adds value.
