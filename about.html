Our story

We saw a gap. We decided to fill it.

By 2020, Scott Deutrom and Keith Rattray had between them spent the better part of four decades inside some of the most significant organisations in UK digital media. They had hired at every level, been placed by search firms, and watched the industry evolve from the earliest days of online and interactive TV through to the programmatic and advanced TV landscape of today.

What they kept seeing was the same problem: a market being served by generalist recruiters who understood CV keywords but not the industry, and larger search firms where senior relationships were built at pitch stage and handed off to junior researchers for the actual work.

Progressive People was founded on a straightforward premise. The people doing the search should be the people who know the market — not in theory, but from having lived and worked inside it. Every search we take is led by one of the two founders, from briefing to offer.

“We only work in the sectors we know deeply. That isn’t a limitation — it’s the point.”

Why a boutique partner

A different model for senior executive search.

Founder-led, every mandate

Every search is run personally by Scott or Keith. There is no junior researcher relay, no offshored qualification call, no associate handover. The person you brief is the person who closes the candidate.

Retained & contingent

We match the engagement model to the brief rather than prescribing every client to the same one. Confidential, market-mapped board and C-suite work is retained; a well-defined hire in a liquid part of the market often need not be, and we will say so.

Short off-limits list

A small client list means a short off-limits list. Most of the candidates a global firm cannot approach for your role, a private headhunter like Progressive People can.

Operator-level fluency

Our founders have actually run commercial teams inside AdTech and digital media businesses. We qualify candidates the way a CRO would, not the way a generalist would.

UK-based, global remits

We are a UK-based practice and the leaders we place are UK or EMEA-based. Their remits are what reach further — single-market, regional and global — with senior placements into New York, Singapore, Paris, Amsterdam, Berlin and Madrid.

Confidential by design

A material proportion of our work is confidential — incumbent replacements, pre-funding hires and competitive replacements. We never speak to the market without explicit, scoped authority from our client.

Why us

What makes us different

We are not a volume recruiter. We are not a generalist firm that happens to work in media. We are a specialist boutique built from the inside out — by people who have lived the industry, understand the roles, and know the talent.

01

Sector depth, not breadth

We only work within AdTech, advanced TV, digital media and MarTech. That focus means every conversation is informed by genuine market knowledge — not a briefing document read the night before.

02
