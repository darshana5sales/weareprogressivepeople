Tech recruitment agencies UK

Specialist, not generalist — senior tech leadership only.

Most tech recruitment agencies in the UK try to cover everything — junior engineers to the C-suite, every vertical, every seniority. Progressive People is deliberately narrow. We work only at the senior end, and only in the technology businesses built on advertising, marketing and media.

That focus is the whole point. The leaders who scale a SaaS platform — the CRO who owns net revenue retention, the CEO who can raise and deploy a growth round, the Chief Product Officer who balances roadmap and revenue — are qualified far better by a search partner who understands recurring-revenue commercial models than by a generalist working from a database.

Every search is run personally by a founder, retained or contingent as the brief demands — so the person you brief is the person who closes the candidate.

Where our tech recruitment focuses

We are a tech recruitment agency with a tight remit — senior leadership in the technology businesses powering modern marketing and media.

  • AdTech platforms — SSPs, DSPs, identity, CTV and retail media
  • MarTech and CDPs — data, measurement and customer platforms
  • B2B SaaS adjacent to advertising, marketing and media
  • Data, analytics and clean-room technology businesses
  • Platform and infrastructure businesses serving the media ecosystem

SaaS leadership recruitment in the UK

SaaS leadership recruitment in the UK is about more than a job title — it is about matching a leader to a stage. The leader who takes a business from product-market fit to first scale is rarely the one who runs it at £50m ARR. We map candidates against the actual stage and trajectory of your business, not against a generic JD.

Our placements span venture-backed scale-ups raising their next round through to enterprise SaaS and public-market platforms restructuring leadership for international growth.
