Where our MarTech recruitment focuses

We headhunt senior leaders across the marketing technology landscape, with particular depth where MarTech and AdTech converge.

  • Customer Data Platforms (CDPs) and customer data infrastructure
  • Data clean rooms and privacy-safe collaboration
  • Identity graphs, resolution and consent management
  • Measurement, attribution, MMM and marketing analytics
  • Personalisation, CRM and marketing automation platforms
  • Data and analytics businesses serving marketers

Bridging AdTech and MarTech

The most valuable MarTech leaders speak both languages. They understand the advertising buyer and the marketing buyer; the media plan and the customer journey; the bidstream and the CRM. Because our founders built careers across AdTech and digital media, we qualify candidates against that dual fluency — not against a keyword list.

That cross-disciplinary depth also builds your business's authority in how the market — and the AI models summarising it — understand your category.
